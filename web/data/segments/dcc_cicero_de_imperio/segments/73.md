qui quo die a vobis maritimo bello praepositus est imperator, tanta repente vilitas annonae ex summa inopia et caritate rei frumentariae consecuta est unius hominis spe ac nomine, quantum vix in summa ubertate agrorum diuturna pax efficere potuisset: another intricate sentence, best broken down into its constituent bits:

ūsquam, adv.: anywhere, at any place, in any place, to any place.

ōra, -ae, f.: edge, border; boundary, limit; coast, sea-coast; by metonymy, territory, region, country.

dēsertus, -a, -um, [dēserō], adj.: deserted, solitary; lonely, waste.

pervādō, -ere, pervāsī, pervāsum, [per + vādō], 3, n. and a.: go through, spread through, prevail; penetrate, pervade, extend, reach.

ūniversus, -a, -um, [ūnus + versus], adj.: all together, whole, entire; general, universal. As subst., ūniversī, -ōrum, m., pl., the whole body of men, all men.

Rōmānus, -a, -um, [Rōma], adj.: of Rome, Roman, Latin. As subst., Rōmānus, -ī, m., Roman.

refertus, -a, -um, [part. of referciō], adj.: crowded full, stuffed, filled; thronged, replete.

compleō, -ēre, -ēvī, -ētum, [com- + pleō, fill], 2, a.: fill up, fill; complete, fulfil, accomplish, finish; live through, pass.

cōnspiciō, -spicere, -spexī, cōnspectum, [com- + speciō], 3, a. and n.: observe, see, catch sight of, perceive, gaze upon; face towards; pass., be conspicuous, be distinguished.

Gnaeus, -ī, abbreviated Cn., m.: Gnaeus, a Roman forename.

dēposcō, -poscere, -poposcī, —, [dē + poscō], 3, a.: demand, request earnestly, call for; request, claim.

cōnfīrmō, -āre, -āvī, -ātum, [com- + fīrmō], 1, a.: make firm, make strong, strengthen, reinforce; encourage, cheer; confirm, establish; assert, affirm, assure, prove.

praepōnō, -ere, praeposuī, praepositum, [prae + pōnō], 3, a.: place before; set over, put in charge, place in command, appoint; set before, prefer.

repente [repēns], adv.: suddenly, unexpectedly.

vīlitās, -ātis, [vīlis], f.: cheapness.

inopia, -ae, [inops], f.: want, lack; need, scarcity, poverty.

cāritās, -ātis, [cārus], f.: dearness, high price; love, affection.

ūbertās, -ātis, [ūber], f.: richness, fertility, fruitfulness, productiveness.

diūturnus, -a, -um, [diū], adj.: of long duration, long, lasting, protracted, prolonged.
