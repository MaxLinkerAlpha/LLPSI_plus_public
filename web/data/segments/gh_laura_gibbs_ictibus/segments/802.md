In prīncipātū commūtandō saepius nīl praeter dominī nomen mūtant pauperēs. Id esse vērum, parva haec fābella indicat. Asellum in prātō timidus pāscēbat senex. Is hostium clāmōre subitō territus suādēbat asinō fugere, nē possent capī. At ille lentus "Quaesō, num bīnās mihi clītellās impositūrum victōrem putās?" Senex negāvit. "Ergō, quid rēfert meā cui serviam, clītellās dum portem meās?"
