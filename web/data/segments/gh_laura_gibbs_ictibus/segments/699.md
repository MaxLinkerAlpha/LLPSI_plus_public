Magnus timor avēs incesserat, nē Scarabaeī arcū pilārī eās occīderent, ā quibus magnam pilārum vim in sterquilīniō summō labōre fābricātam audīerant. Tunc passer: "Nōlīte (inquit) expavēscere. Quō modō enim pilās in nōs per āera volantēs iacere potuērunt, cum eās per terram magnō mōlīmine vix trahant?" Haec fābula nōs admonet nē hostium opēs extimēscāmus, quibus dēesse vidēmus ingenium.
