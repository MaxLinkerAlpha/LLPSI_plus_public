Tū cūr ōva locō paris ūnō Pīca Columbae, Exclūdīque solent hīc ea semper, ait? An nōn ipsa parās prūdentior esse rapīnīs, Quās hominum fūrtō cōgeris ūsque patī? Mē mea simplicitās (perhibent dīxisse Columbam) Impedit, haec patiēns tālia fūrta ferō. Simplicitās haud ipsa nocēns obnoxia fraudī est, Quam tamen indignē nōn ea ferre solet.
