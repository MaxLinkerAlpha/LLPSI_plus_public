Sustinet ingentēs tōtō quōs ēdere rictū Rāna, sonōs pavitāns hausit ut aure Leō: Perculsum perhibent clāmōribus ācriter illīs, Ēdere quōs magnam crēdidit ipse feram. Mox animō redeunte tamen circumspicit audāx, Inque hostem positō sē parat ille metū. At dē vīcīnō repentem flūmine Rānam Observāns, temerē sē stupuisse videt. Concipit hinc īram pariter, pariterque pudōrem, Iamque domāns Rānam calce premente necat. Quōlibet est strepitū rēs digna pudōre monērī, Explōrāta parum turpe timēre virō.
