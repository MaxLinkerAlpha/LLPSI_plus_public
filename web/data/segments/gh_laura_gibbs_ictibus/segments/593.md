Leō, rēx quādrupedum, adversus volucrēs pugnātūrus, suōrum aciēs īnstruēbat. Interrogātus autem ab ursō, quid eī asinī inertiā aut leporis timiditas ad victōriam cōnferre possent, quōs ibi inter cēterōs mīlitēs adesse cernēbat, respondit: asinus tubae suae clangōre mīlitēs ad pugnam concitābit, lepus vērō ob pedum celeritātem tabellariī fungētur officiō. Fābula significat nēminem adeō contemptibilem quī aliquā rē nōbīs prōdesse nōn possit.
