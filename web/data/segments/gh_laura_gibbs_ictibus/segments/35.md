Cervam īnsequēbātur Leō: illa fugā ēvādit, dēnsōque quōdam abdit sē in nemore. Leō vērō, quī, quantum rōbore Cervam vincit, tantum vēlōcitāte ā Cervā vincitur, nemus adit, quaeritque ex Pāstōre, sīcubi latitantem vīdisset Cervam. Negat Pāstōr, simulque manū prōtēnsā locum ostendit; atque ita Cervam miserrimam invādit Leō. Vulpēcula autem, Nae tū, inquit Pāstōrī, imbellis simul improbusque es: cōram Leōne quidem imbellis, improbus vērō in Cervam.
