Aequārī vult rāna bovī, tumet ergō; tumentī Nātus ait: "Cessā, prae bove tōta nihil." Rāna dolet meliusque tumet; premit ille tumentem: "Vincere nōn poteris, victa crepāre potes." Tertius īrātam vexat tumor; illa tumōris Cōpia findit eam; guttura rupta patent. Cum maiōre minor cōnferrī dēsinat et sē Cōnsulat et vīrēs temperet ipse suās.
