Figulus quīdam multās avēs in officīnā fōrmāverat. Asinus vērō praeteriēns, Agāsōne negligenter sequente, per fenestram īnsertō capite avēs illās ēvertit, et vāsa, quae erant in officīnā, cōnfrēgit. Cum igitur dominus officīnae Agāsōnem in iūdicium addūceret, ille ab obviīs interrogātus, cuius crīminis causa in iūs vocārētur, respondit: Ob Asinum quī in officīnam intrōspēxit.
