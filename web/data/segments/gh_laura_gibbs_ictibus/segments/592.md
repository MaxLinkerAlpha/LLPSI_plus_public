Dum nive candet humus, glaciēs dum sōpit aquārum Cursūs, in colubrum turbida saevit hiems. Hunc videt, hunc reficit hominis clēmentia; ventum Temperat huic tēctō, temperat igne gelū. Ōre serit vīrus coluber, sīc toxicat aedem. Hospes ait colubrō: Nōn reditūrus abī. Nōn exit coluber nec vult exīre, sed haeret Amplectēnsque virum sībila dīra movet. Reddere gaudet homō nēquam prō melle venēnum, Prō frūctū poenam, prō pietāte dolum.
